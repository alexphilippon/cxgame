         Regulations – X²O Badkamers Trophy Cyclo-cross 2026–2027
Art. 1
The X²O Badkamers Trofee is organised by Golazo Sports, in cooperation with
the non-profit associations Sportinez vzw and Fietspromo vzw, and is run in
accordance with the applicable UCI and KBWB regulations.
Art. 2
All riders who, under UCI/KBWB regulations, are entitled to compete in A-
category cyclo-cross races are eligible for the overall classification of the Elite
Men and Elite Women.

Art. 4
An overall classification is established based on time.
The classification is drawn up on the basis of the riders’ finishing times, in the
same way as in a road stage race. Riders may also earn time bonuses (see Art.
5).
If a rider finishes with a time deficit of more than five minutes, is taken out of
the race under the 80% rule, or abandons the race, a penalty of five minutes
will be added to the time of the winner of that round.
These five minutes do not include any bonus seconds possibly earned during
the intermediate sprint or for the fastest lap.
If a rider does not start in one of the rounds of the X²O Badkamers Trophy, a
penalty of five minutes will be added to his/her classification time, based on
the time of the winner of that round.
For the final overall classification of all categories, all times from the 8 races are
taken into account.
In the event of a tie on time (hours, minutes, seconds), the rider’s best result in
the X²O Badkamers Trophy will be decisive. If this is also identical, the result in
the final round will determine the ranking.
Hundredths of a second are not taken into account.
Art. 5 – Time Bonuses
Intermediate Sprint
At each round of the regularity competition, an intermediate sprint is organised
at the finish line at the end of the first lap. Riders can earn the following bonus
seconds for the overall classification:
   •   Winner of the intermediate sprint: 15 seconds
   •   Second place: 10 seconds
   •   Third place: 5 seconds

Fastest Lap
At each round, riders can also earn bonus seconds based on the fastest lap
completed during the race (the starting lap is not included). The riders with the
fastest laps receive:
   •   Fastest lap: 15 seconds
   •   Second fastest lap: 10 seconds
   •   Third fastest lap: 5 seconds

These bonuses are not cumulative; a rider may only receive one fastest-lap
time bonus per race.

Art. 7
The first three riders in the results of each race must report to the podium after
the race for the award ceremony.
The leader of the overall classification must also report to the podium after
each race for the ceremony and make him-/herself available to the press.
Art. 8
After each race, an intermediate classification will be drawn up and published
on the X²O Badkamers Trofee website.
The final overall classification will be published after the last race on the official
website: www.trofeeveldrijden.be.
For all categories, a time classification applies.
Only riders who finish within five minutes of the winner of a round are included
in the intermediate and final overall classifications of the X²O Badkamers
Trofee.
Art. 9
A riders’ village will be provided for all riders in the categories Elite Men, Under
23, and Elite Women.
All riders are required to park their respective vehicles/campers in this area in
accordance with the applicable organisational regulations.
The organisation provides priority parking for (Pro-)Continental teams that
have a reserved space via their KBWB parking stickers (subject to availability).
Individual riders receive their parking pass upon registration.
Art. 10
The riders finishing 1st, 2nd, and 3rd in the elite race are required, if requested
by the organisation, to report to the VIP area after the podium ceremony for a
short interview.
This is organised by the press officer in consultation with the organisation.
If the riders finishing 1st, 2nd, or 3rd refuse to participate, a fine of €500 will be
imposed.
Art. 11
Any cases not provided for in these regulations shall be decided by the Steering
Committee.
