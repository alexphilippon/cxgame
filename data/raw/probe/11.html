Tour de France 2027: Route and stages

The Grand Départ of the 2027 Tour de France is going to take place on Friday 2 July in Edinburgh. The first three stages will take the peloton through Scotland, England, and Wales. It will be the third time that the UK hosts the biggest cycling race on the planet, after the Big Starts in London (2007) and Yorkshire (2014). After three stages in Great Britain, Le Tour continues on French soil before reaching its climax in the Pyrenees with the queen stage to the Col du Portet after three weeks of action. Le Tour will finish on Sunday 25 July in Paris.

Giro 2027: Route & rumours

Next year’s Giro sets off from Trieste on Saturday 8 May. Stage 1 takes the riders partly through Slovenia, while the 2nd stage finishes in Pordenone. The pink caravan is then rumoured to head south along the Adriatic coast before crossing the Apennines to the Tyrrhenian coast and making its way north again through Tuscany, Liguria and Piedmont. The final week is expected to feature the Zoncolan, Passo Gavia and a double Mortirolo ascent.

Tour de France 2027: Route

The 2027 Tour de France sets off from Edinburgh, Scotland, on 2 July. The opening week features a mix of sprint stages and opportunities for punchers. The Alps are on the menu in the second week, followed by the Pyrenees in the third, while the route also includes two individual time trials. Over a total distance of around 3,500 kilometres, the riders tackle a total of 53,450 metres of elevation gain.
